1. Motion

Motion is the change in the position of an object with time. Objects around us can move in different ways. A car moving on a road, a ball rolling on the ground, and a person walking are examples of motion. Physics helps us understand the speed, direction, and distance of moving objects.

2. Force

Force is a push or pull that can change the motion of an object. It can make an object start moving, stop, speed up, slow down, or change direction. For example, pushing a door or kicking a ball involves force. Force is an important concept used to understand how objects interact with each other.

3. Energy

Energy is the ability to do work. It exists in many forms, including heat, light, sound, electrical, and chemical energy. We use energy every day for cooking, lighting, transportation, and operating machines. Physics helps us understand how energy is transferred and changed from one form to another.

4. Light

Light is a form of energy that allows us to see objects around us. It travels very quickly and can travel through empty space. Light can be reflected, refracted, or absorbed by different materials. Mirrors, lenses, cameras, and many modern technologies work because of the properties of light.

5. Sound

Sound is a form of energy produced by vibrations. It travels through a medium such as air, water, or solids. We hear sound when vibrations reach our ears. Music, speech, and sounds from machines are common examples. Physics helps us understand how sound is produced, travels, and is heard.
