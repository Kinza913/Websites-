1. Matter

Matter is anything that has mass and occupies space. It is found everywhere around us in different forms. The three common states of matter are solid, liquid, and gas. Water, air, stones, and books are examples of matter. Chemistry helps us understand the properties and changes of matter.

2. Atoms

An atom is the basic unit of an element. Everything around us is made of atoms. An atom contains smaller particles called protons, neutrons, and electrons. Different types of atoms combine in different ways to form substances. The study of atoms helps us understand how materials behave.

3. Elements

An element is a pure substance made up of only one type of atom. There are many elements, such as oxygen, hydrogen, carbon, and iron. Each element has its own properties and chemical symbol. Elements are arranged in the periodic table according to their atomic structure and properties.

4. Compounds

A compound is a substance formed when two or more different elements combine chemically. Water is a common example because it is made from hydrogen and oxygen. Compounds have properties that can be different from the elements that form them. Chemistry helps us understand how compounds are formed and used.

5. Chemical Reactions

A chemical reaction happens when substances change into new substances. Chemical reactions occur in many parts of everyday life. Cooking food, rusting iron, and burning fuel are examples. During a reaction, atoms are rearranged to form new substances. Chemistry helps us understand and explain these changes.
